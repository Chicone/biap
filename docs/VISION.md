# BIAP Vision

## Biomedical Image Analysis Platform (BIAP)

BIAP is an open-source AI platform for quantitative biomedical image analysis.

The project aims to provide an end-to-end framework for analysing microscopy and biomedical imaging data by combining modern computer vision, machine learning, graph neural networks (GNNs), large language models (LLMs), and, ultimately, agentic AI.

Rather than being a collection of isolated tools, BIAP is designed as a modular scientific platform that supports reproducible experiments, interpretable AI models, and interactive exploration of experimental data.

The long-term goal is to develop software resembling the AI platforms used in modern pharmaceutical companies and biomedical research institutes, demonstrating how computer vision, scientific computing, and AI can be integrated into a unified research workflow.

## Core Principles

- Modular architecture
- Reproducible scientific workflows
- Explainable and interpretable AI
- Experiment-driven design
- Modern software engineering practices
- Open-source and extensible
